# Getting the houses in, by talking

He does not need to fill anything in. He records a voice note, his phone
transcribes it, and the transcript gets turned into the import sheet.

## How to record it

Any of these transcribe automatically — no app to install:

- **iPhone** — Voice Memos (iOS 18+ shows a transcript), or Notes › new note ›
  microphone on the keyboard
- **Android** — Google Recorder, or the keyboard microphone in any notes app
- **Either** — a WhatsApp voice note to himself, then tap the transcript

Best time is in the ute between sites, or on the couch with the list in front of
him. It runs about eight minutes for twenty-five houses.

## What to say for each house

Roughly twenty seconds each. Say them in any order and stop and start as much as
he likes — a messy transcript is fine, because a person reads it, not a parser.

> **"Fourteen Wattle Grove, Kellyville. Double storey. Priya and Arun Raman.
> We're on the roof and the bricklayers start next week. Waiting on the windows,
> should be here Thursday. Handover looking like March, April next year.
> Started February."**

That covers everything:

| He says | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| Street address and suburb | Identifies the house |
| Single or double storey | Shown on the owner's page |
| Owner names | Who it belongs to — **first names alone are fine** |
| Where it's up to | Sets the stage, and everything before it to complete |
| Anything holding it up | Becomes the first line the owner reads |
| Handover guess | Shown as a range, never a single date |
| When it started | Estimates the stages behind the current one |

**Skip anything he doesn't know.** A house with just an address and a stage is
still worth loading — the rest can be filled in later.

**Stage names don't have to be exact.** "Brickwork", "we're bricking", "walls
are going up" all land on the same stage. He should use his own words.

**Multiple things at once is normal and fine.** "Roof's on and the brickies have
started" records both as underway, which is how the site actually works.

## The one thing that can't come from a voice note

**Owner email addresses.** "Dot com dot a u" against "dotcomau" is a silent
failure, and the failure mode is that owner never hearing anything — precisely
what this whole system exists to prevent.

So houses load without them. Get the emails from somewhere checkable
afterwards — his contacts, the contracts, or a forwarded email thread — and add
them separately. Until an owner has an email they simply cannot sign in, and the
import will tell you how many are outstanding.

This is also convenient: everything can be loaded and inspected while no owner
can see a thing.

## What happens next

1. Paste the transcript over.
2. It comes back as a table — one row per house, every detail laid out to check.
3. Anything unclear gets asked about rather than guessed.
4. Once it's confirmed, it imports, and the site is live for whoever has an
   email attached.
